# Anti-Slop Human Accessibility Audit — `docs/DESIGN.md` (`audit-001-2026-11-16`)

Audited target: [`docs/DESIGN.md`](../docs/DESIGN.md) and its referenced base stylesheet, document shell, domain palettes, and shared UI primitives (`index.html`, `src/index.css`, `src/layouts/MainLayout.tsx`, `src/components/`, `src/types/member.ts`, `src/features/batches/api/recognitionMetadata.ts`, `src/features/statistics/`).

Contrast ratios verified with `antislop-human/contrast-check.py` (WCAG 2.x relative luminance).

---

## Numbered Findings

1. **[HIGH | R-25]** Uncapped muted neutral opacities (`text-neutral/60` = `3.55:1`, `text-neutral/50` = `2.75:1` on `#FFFFFF`) and `placeholder-primary/40` (`#CEB1A6` on `bg-primary/5` `#F9F6F5` = `1.87:1`) fail WCAG AA `4.5:1` normal text, while `docs/DESIGN.md` specifies no minimum passing opacity floor (`neutral/70` = `4.68:1` is the lightest passing step).
2. **[HIGH | R-25]** `--color-tertiary` (`#6CB7DE`) in `docs/DESIGN.md` Section 1 reaches only `2.22:1` with white (`FAIL` normal and large text) vs. `5.30:1` with `#383836`, and data-viz `sky-600` (`#0284C7`) reaches `4.10:1` on `#FFFFFF` (`FAIL` normal text; `sky-700` `#0369A1` = `5.93:1`), with no foreground/background pairing rules documented.
3. **[HIGH | R-25]** Recognition badge `"Campeones por la Naturaleza"` (`#C2410C` on `#FEE2D8`, `src/features/batches/api/recognitionMetadata.ts:37`, documented in `docs/DESIGN.md` Section 5) measures `4.21:1` (`FAIL` `4.5:1` for `text-xs` badge text).
4. **[HIGH | R-25]** `docs/DESIGN.md` Sections 3–4 standardize interactive component borders on `border-primary/20` (`#E8DCD7` on `#FFFFFF` = `1.34:1`), `border-gray-200` (`#E5E7EB` = `1.24:1`), and `border-gray-300` (`#D1D5DB` = `1.47:1`), all failing WCAG 1.4.11 `3.0:1` non-text contrast for `Field` inputs and `outline` buttons (`border-primary/70` `#AF8373` = `3.32:1`; `gray-500` `#6B7280` = `4.83:1`).
5. **[HIGH | R-25]** Statistics chart and progress fills documented in `docs/DESIGN.md` Section 5 fail `3.0:1` non-text contrast on white backgrounds—`slate-300` (`#CBD5E1` = `1.48:1`), `emerald-400` (`#34D399` = `1.92:1`), `amber-500` (`#F59E0B` = `2.15:1`), `emerald-500` (`#10B981` = `2.54:1`), `slate-400` (`#94A3B8` = `2.56:1`)—without high-contrast borders, patterns, or paired labels specified.
6. **[HIGH | R-25]** `docs/DESIGN.md` Section 1 defines the multi-stop hero gradient (`#170702` → `#2E1208` → `rgba(140, 78, 55, 0.5)` → transparent) behind the negative white World Scouting logo (`HeroSection.tsx`) without specifying a minimum solid scrim footprint or worst-spot `>= 3:1` / `>= 4.5:1` verification across mobile and desktop viewports.
7. **[HIGH | R-32 / R-34]** `docs/DESIGN.md` mentions `focus:ring-2` only on `Button` and omits a global `:focus-visible` indicator standard (`>= 3:1` contrast) and `outline: none` ban, leaving header/sidebar buttons (e.g., `Sidebar.tsx` collapse and settings triggers) and links without a documented or enforced keyboard focus ring.
8. **[HIGH | R-32 / R-26]** `docs/DESIGN.md` Section 4 documents `Modal.tsx` styling and animations without specifying modal focus trapping, `Escape` dismissal, or focus restoration (`Modal.tsx` renders `<dialog open>` with an unfocusable `onKeyDown` on the backdrop `<div>`, and `Sidebar.tsx` mobile `role="dialog"` omits focus trapping and `Escape`).
9. **[HIGH | R-32 / R-39]** `docs/DESIGN.md` defines Spanish UI copy (`Section 2`) and app shell navigation (`Section 4`) without specifying document-level accessibility semantics: `index.html:2` declares `<html lang="en">` instead of `lang="es"`, and `MainLayout.tsx` omits a `"Saltar al contenido principal"` skip link targeting `<main id="main-content" tabindex="-1">`.
10. **[HIGH | R-39]** `docs/DESIGN.md` and `src/index.css` `@layer base` omit the pointer-cursor base layer (`cursor: pointer` on enabled interactive elements, `cursor: not-allowed` on `:disabled` / `[aria-disabled="true"]`), leaving Tailwind Preflight `button` controls (`Button.tsx`) with an arrow cursor.
11. **[HIGH | R-39 / C-4]** `docs/DESIGN.md` omits accessibility requirements for status/error feedback, icon semantics, and reduced motion: (a) `Field` errors (`Field.tsx` lacks `aria-invalid`/`aria-describedby`/`role="alert"`) and Section 6 #7 unified toasts lack mandatory non-color icons and `aria-live` regions; (b) icons inside labeled/icon-only buttons lack `aria-hidden="true"` and `aria-label` rules; and (c) `prefers-reduced-motion` is documented only for `Modal`, excluding `animate-pulse`, `animate-fade-in`, and chart/hover transitions.
12. **[HIGH | R-27]** `docs/DESIGN.md` covers resting component tokens only and lacks visual and accessible pattern contracts for mandatory data-view **Loading** (`aria-busy` / descriptive status), **Empty** (explanatory Spanish guidance), and **Error** (`role="alert"` + retry action) states.
13. **[HIGH | R-35]** `docs/DESIGN.md` Type Scale and Primitives permit sub-`12px` micro text (`text-[10px]` / `text-[11px]`) and `overflow-hidden` / `truncate` wrappers (`Card`, `Modal`, `Table`, `StatKpiGrid`) without specifying fluid `rem` zoom rules or `200%` text-resize non-clipping safeguards.
14. **[MEDIUM | R-06]** `docs/DESIGN.md` Section 2 enforces `uppercase` + `tracking-wider` / `tracking-wide` on table headers (`text-xs`) and form `Field` labels, reducing word-shape legibility for multi-word Spanish labels without a documented readability exception.
